Job Title: AI Engineer Intern

We are looking for an AI Engineer Intern to support our team in building
and experimenting with machine learning and generative AI features.

Requirements:
- 0 to 1 years of general software or AI-related experience (coursework,
  personal projects, or internships all count).
- Working knowledge of Python.
- Basic familiarity with machine learning and deep learning concepts
  (e.g. through coursework, tutorials, or personal projects).
- Willingness and enthusiasm to learn new tools and techniques on the job.
- Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree in Computer Science,
  Data Science, AI, or a related field.
- Familiarity with Git is preferred.
- Exposure to any deep learning framework (PyTorch or TensorFlow) is
  preferred but not required.
- Prior personal or academic project involving AI/ML is preferred.

Compensation: $70,000 USD annually.

Responsibilities:
- Assist with data preprocessing, model training, and evaluation under
  guidance from senior engineers.
- Contribute to small features and experiments in existing AI-powered
  services.
- Write clean, readable Python code and learn our team's testing practices.
- Participate in code reviews and team discussions to build production
  ML experience.
